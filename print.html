Community Outreach · Give Back Project

Need a hand? Just ask.

Free help for older adults in Rocky Hill and nearby towns, from neighbors at our church. Prayer requests always welcome.

Scan with your phone's camera

We can help with

  • Phones, tablets, and computers
  • Email, texting, and video calls
  • Online forms and patient portals
  • Spotting scams
  • Paperwork and letters
  • Light chores and errands
  • A friendly visit
  • Prayer, for anyone, about anything

How to ask

  • Visit
  • Or email
  • Or call
  • Or tell any member of our church
  • Or visit us at
