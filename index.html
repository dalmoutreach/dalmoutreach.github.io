Good to know

What we can't do

We're church volunteers, not a licensed company, so some jobs are beyond us. If you're not sure, ask. If we can't help, we'll try to point you to someone who can.

  • Building, remodeling, roofing, or other major repairs
  • Electrical, plumbing, or heating work
  • Medical care or giving medication
  • Legal, tax, or financial advice
  • Paying bills or giving money
  • Emergencies: if you are in danger, call 911

For families & caregivers

Worried about a parent or neighbor?

You can ask for help on someone's behalf, even if you live out of state. A few things that make it go smoothly:

  1. Talk to them first. People are happier to accept help they've agreed to. Let them know a church volunteer may call.
  2. Put your number down too. Mention it in the details so we can reach you if they don't pick up.
  3. Be specific. "Help Mom join her doctor's video visits" lets us send the right volunteer.
  4. Share our safety promise. We never ask for money, passwords, or bank details. Anyone who does isn't us.

Questions

Common questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Our help is always free. We will never ask you for money.

Can I just ask for prayer?

Yes, please do. Prayer requests are always welcome, from anyone, about anything. Use the prayer request form. You can leave your name off, and you can ask for someone to call and pray with you.

Do I have to belong to the church?

No. Anyone can ask for help, whatever their faith or background. If you'd ever like to visit, we'd love to see you at 623 Old Main Street in Rocky Hill, or watch online.

Can I ask on behalf of my parent or neighbor?

Yes. Choose "Someone else" on the form and tell us a little about them. Please make sure they're happy for us to get in touch.

What happens after I send a request?

Someone from our ministry team reads every request. A volunteer will reach out the way you asked, by phone, text, or email, to talk it through and arrange a time.

Where do you help?

We're based in Rocky Hill, so we mainly help people in Rocky Hill and nearby towns like Wethersfield, Cromwell, Newington, Glastonbury, and Berlin. If you're farther away, ask anyway. We can often help over the phone, and we'll gladly pray for anyone, anywhere.

How do I know a volunteer is really from you?

Our volunteers will tell you they're from Dimension Abundant Life Ministries and mention the request you sent. You can always check by calling the church office at 860.604.5250. They will never ask for money, passwords, or bank details.
