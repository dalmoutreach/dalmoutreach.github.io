Helpful numbers for Rocky Hill & Connecticut

Trusted places to call. All of them are free. On a phone, tap a number to call it.

Right now

If someone is in danger or in crisis.

Emergency

Police, fire, or ambulance. If you or someone else is in danger.

911

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

If you or someone you love is struggling, feeling hopeless, or thinking about suicide. You can call or text.

988 24 hours a day

Right here in Rocky Hill

Our town has great services for older adults. These are good first calls.

Rocky Hill Senior Center

Classes, meals, trips, and friendship. Free membership for Rocky Hill residents. 55 Church Street.

860-258-2786

Rocky Hill Human, Youth & Senior Services

Help with heating and energy bills, emergency basic needs, food, renters' tax relief, and the town mini-bus for residents 60 and older. 761 Old Main Street.

860-258-2799 Weekdays, 8:30 am – 4:30 pm

Cora J. Belden Library

Free computers, Wi-Fi, large-print books, and friendly staff who can help you get started online. 33 Church Street.

860-258-7621

Rocky Hill Police (non-emergency)

To report a suspicious caller or scam in town, or for a wellness check on someone. For emergencies, always call 911.

860-258-7640 699 Old Main Street

Everyday help

Food, heating bills, housing, rides, and finding local services.

211 Connecticut (United Way)

One call for local help: heating and utility bills, food, housing, transportation, and much more. They'll point you to the right place.

211 24 hours a day · From outside CT: 1-800-203-1234

Operation Fuel

Year-round help with energy and heating bills for Connecticut households who are struggling to pay.

860-243-2345

Eldercare Locator

A national service that connects older adults and caregivers to local support, like meals, home care, and transportation.

1-800-677-1116

Scams and fraud

If something feels wrong, or you think you've lost money.

AARP Fraud Watch Helpline

Free help from trained volunteers if you've been targeted, or aren't sure whether something is a scam. You don't need to be an AARP member.

877-908-3360 Weekdays, 8 am – 8 pm

CT Department of Consumer Protection

Report scams, bad contractors, and unfair business practices in Connecticut.

1-800-842-2649 Complaints line: (860) 713-6300

Federal Trade Commission

Report a scam online so it can be tracked and stopped. A family member can help you fill it in.

ReportFraud.ftc.gov

Medicare and benefits

Questions about health coverage or Social Security.

CHOICES: free Medicare counseling

Connecticut's free, unbiased Medicare help. A counselor can compare plans with you. They don't sell insurance.

1-800-994-9422

Medicare

Questions about your Medicare card, coverage, or bills.

1-800-633-4227 1-800-MEDICARE · 24 hours a day

Social Security

Benefits, payments, replacement cards, and changes of address.

1-800-772-1213

Safety and protection

If an older adult is being hurt, neglected, or taken advantage of, including by someone they know.

Protective Services for the Elderly

Connecticut's Department of Social Services. Report suspected abuse, neglect, or financial exploitation of someone 60 or older.

1-888-385-4225 Weekdays, 8 am – 4:30 pm · After hours, dial 211

Elder Justice Hotline

Run by the Connecticut Attorney General's office, for reporting abuse, neglect, and financial exploitation.

860-808-5555

We last checked these numbers in October 2026. If one doesn't work, dial 211 and they'll help you find the right place, or ask us.
